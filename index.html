How to use the map

  1. ●
    Choose an MRT line

    Click a coloured line button to focus on that route.

  2. ♫
    Choose a station

    Click a station name. You will hear the station announced aloud.

  3. ✦
    Follow the glowing line

    The selected route will glow to help you follow it.

  4. ↗
    See the whole network again

    Click “Back to full MRT map” to return to all lines.
